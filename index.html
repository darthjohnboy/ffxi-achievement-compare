How the points work

  • Jobs: 1 point per level + 100 per job at 99.
  • Master Levels: 1 point per Master Level.
  • Crafts: 2 points per level up to 70 per craft + 1 bonus point per level over 70.
  • Relics / ultimates: 250 per 119, 150 per 99-or-below REMA, 50 per extra (fishing rod, escutcheon).
  • Titles: quest 10, mission 20, NM 25, battlefield 50, Master Trials / endgame 100, ungrouped 0.
  • Checklist: Records of Eminence 5, missions 5, quests 10, warps 5, key items 2 — per completed item.
  • Title badges: 100 / 250 / 500 / 750 / 1,000 titles.

Baseline jobs, crafts and relics are FFXIAH snapshots from 2026-11-15 and only change when someone re-scrapes. Synced data comes from the XIchecklist autosync fork via the Google Form receiver.
